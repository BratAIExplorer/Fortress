The one rule that decides everything

Does the fund hold US stocks?
→ Yes: Ireland wins. Ireland has a US tax treaty — only 15% of US dividends get taken. Singapore and Hong Kong have no US treaty, so their funds lose the full 30%. Same stocks, double the tax.
→ No (Asia-only): local wins. For STI, Hang Seng, S-REITs or China A-shares there is no US tax at all — so an SG or HK fund is cleanest, cheapest, and avoids currency conversion.
